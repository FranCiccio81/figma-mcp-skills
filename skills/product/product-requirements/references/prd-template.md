## Review checklist

- Problem is stated without the solution.
- Impact is quantified or explicitly unknown.
- Each requirement is testable and atomic.
- Accessibility, privacy, error states, empty states and offline behaviour are covered.
- Analytics events needed to measure success are listed.
- Copy and localization owners are named.
- Dependencies on other teams are explicit.
